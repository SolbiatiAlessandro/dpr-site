Community

Rinpoche founded Nalandabodhi, an international community of Buddhist centers, and Nitartha International, which preserves and teaches the Buddhist literature of Tibet. Both welcome newcomers in person and online.

Nalandabodhi

Rinpoche’s international community of Buddhist study and practice centers, founded 1997; he is founder, president, and spiritual director. Website →

Nitartha International

Non-profit educational corporation founded by Rinpoche in 1994, headquartered in Seattle, preserving endangered Asian texts and art in digital formats — tagline 'Ancient Wisdom for the Modern Mind'. Website →

Nitartha Institute

Division of Nitartha International established in 1996: a systematic program of higher Buddhist studies adapting the shedra curriculum for Western students, integrated with meditation. Website →

Nalanda West

“Center for American Buddhism” in Seattle, opened 2003 as the primary seat of Rinpoche’s activities in North America; venue for Tara Drupchen and Sunday meditation series. Website →

Nitartha Digital Library

Nitartha International’s online library of Tibetan texts (site title in Tibetan; redirects to /xtf/search). Website →

Karmapa Center 16

Center near Zion, IL commemorating the 16th Karmapa, building his Parinirvana Stupa Temple; Rinpoche led the Guru Rinpoche statue consecration there in July 2026. Website →

Karmapa Foundation

Nonprofit Rinpoche led the formation of after his 2007 appointment as US representative of the Karmapa’s Office of Administration; he serves as its president (per biography). Website →

Dzogchen Monastery

Nyingma seat in eastern Tibet founded 1685; the Dzogchen Ponlop line has served among its main abbots since the 17th century. Website →

About Dzogchen Ponlop Rinpoche

Dzogchen Ponlop Rinpoche is a widely known Buddhist teacher and the author of Emotional Rescue, Rebel Buddha, and other books. A lover of classic rock music, art, and urban culture, he is a poet, photographer, accomplished calligrapher, and visual artist. Ponlop Rinpoche is the founder, president, and spiritual director of Nalandabodhi, an international community of Buddhist centers, and of Nitartha Institute for Higher Buddhist Studies.

Rinpoche is one of the foremost scholars and meditation masters of his generation in the Nyingma and Kagyu schools of Tibetan Buddhism. He is known for his sharp intellect, humor, and easygoing teaching style, for launching the kindness initiative #GoKind, and for his outreach to communities internationally.
